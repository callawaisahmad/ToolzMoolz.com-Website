How to Convert PDF to Excel Online Free

Our browser-based PDF to Excel converter makes it simple to extract tables, numerical listings, and database blocks from digital PDF documents and convert them into fully editable spreadsheets. When working with PDF lists, copying and pasting data manually ruins the spacing and columns. By utilizing high-fidelity local text-coordinate extraction (leveraging PDF.js), this tool groups line fragments by their coordinates, automatically populating an interactive grid editor. Once parsed, you can tweak, append, or delete row values before exporting directly as clean Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) or CSV files.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Select File: Click "Choose PDF File" or drag & drop any native PDF document under 25MB.
  2. Automatic Parsing: The browser's local parser immediately reads all page characters, grouping them into rows and columns by matching vertical text alignments.
  3. Review & Edit: Review the extracted data inside the preview panel. Click on any table cell to manually update details, or tap **Add Row** to input new entries.
  4. Save & Export: Click **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV** to save the reviewed tabular data to your local download folder instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my data kept safe and secure?

Yes, 100%. Our tool is fully serverless. All document readings, coordinate mappings, table assemblies, and file generations are handled client-side inside your own browser window. Your sensitive corporate details, invoices, or customer databases are never sent over the network.

Why does the table look blank or show spacing offsets?

This tool extracts text characters directly from digital (native) PDFs. Scanned PDFs (pure images without text layers) or highly complex double-column brochures may not line up perfectly. In these cases, you can simply click any cell in our interactive grid editor and manually edit the values in seconds.

Is there a file page limit?

There is no hardcoded page limit. Since processing occurs in your local system memory, documents under 30 pages parse almost instantly; larger spreadsheets may take slightly longer depending on your device speed.
