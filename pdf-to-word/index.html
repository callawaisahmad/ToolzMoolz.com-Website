How to Convert PDF to Word Online Free

Need to edit a PDF but only have a locked-down copy? Our PDF to Word converter extracts the selectable text from any digital PDF and rebuilds it as a clean, editable .docx document you can open in Microsoft Word or Google Docs. Everything runs locally in your browser using PDF.js, so files are never uploaded. This is ideal for turning read-only contracts, reports and policy documents into editable drafts, quotes, and teaching materials — without paying for Adobe or installing anything.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Select File: Click "Choose PDF File" or drag and drop your document.
  2. Automatic Extraction: The tool reads every page and fills the editable text panel.
  3. Review & Edit: Tweak headings, fix typos or reorder paragraphs before exporting.
  4. Save & Export: Click .docx for a Word document or TXT for plain text.

Why convert a PDF to Word?

PDFs are fixed, so editing one normally means retyping it. Converting to Word gives you the selectable text back as an editable .docx you can open in Microsoft Word or Google Docs, then reword, restyle or merge into another document. It is the fastest way to turn a read-only contract, report or policy into a working draft.

Converting PDFs to Word cleanly

  • Proofread the layout afterwards; spacing, tabs and tables can move around.
  • Fonts that are not installed on your computer are substituted, which can change line breaks.
  • Images stay as images — they are not turned into editable text.
  • This tool reads selectable text, so a scanned page needs OCR before conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the converted Word document editable?

Yes. The .docx output is a real Microsoft Word document you can edit, style and save normally in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Only digital PDFs with selectable text extract cleanly. For scanned (image) documents, use the OCR Text Extractor on ToolzMoolz first.

Is this PDF to Word converter free?

Yes, completely free with no limits, watermarks or registration required.
