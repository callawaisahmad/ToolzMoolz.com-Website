How to use the PDF Studio

Choose a tool from the tabs above: Compress to shrink file size, Merge to combine several PDFs into one, Split to pull out selected pages, or Extract Images to grab every embedded picture as a PNG. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Is it private and secure?

Yes. Your PDF files are processed entirely on your device using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Nothing you upload is ever sent to, tracked or stored on any server.
