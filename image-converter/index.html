How to use the Free Batch Image Converter

Pick your target format (WEBP for the best balance of size and quality, AVIF for the newest, most compact next-gen format, JPEG for maximum compatibility with email and older software, or PNG for fully lossless quality with transparency). Then drag and drop as many images as you like into the upload area. Use the quality slider to balance file size and visual fidelity, the resize options to shrink or enlarge a whole batch (e.g. to 50% or to a 1200px max width), and pick a background color to fill transparent areas when exporting to JPEG. Hit Convert & Download Images — a single image downloads directly, multiple images are bundled into one ZIP.

Why convert images in bulk?

Batch conversion saves hours when you need to standardize a folder of product photos, website assets, or social media images. Designers, web developers, photographers and e-commerce sellers use this free bulk image converter to switch dozens of files between JPG, PNG and WebP at once — no paid desktop software and no per-image fees. Whether you're exporting WebP assets for a faster WordPress site, converting PNG screenshots to smaller JPEGs, or turning JPEGs into transparent PNGs, everything happens instantly in your browser.

Is it really free & private?

Yes. This batch image converter is 100% free with no limits and no watermarks. All conversion happens inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and JSZip — nothing you upload is ever sent to, tracked or stored on any server. Your images never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this batch image converter free?

Yes — completely free forever. No signup, no registration, no watermark, no file count limits and no premium tier. Convert as many images as you need, as often as you want.

How many images can I convert at once?

As many as your browser can handle. Drag and drop a whole folder of images and convert them all in one batch — the results download together as a single ZIP file.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every image is converted locally on your device using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your files are never uploaded, making this the most private free batch image converter available.

Which format should I choose?

WebP for the smallest files and modern websites (25–35% smaller than JPG with identical quality), JPEG/JPG for the widest compatibility with email clients and older software, and PNG for lossless graphics that need crisp transparency.

Can I convert without losing quality?

Yes — choose PNG for fully lossless output, or keep the quality slider at 90–100% for near-lossless JPG and WebP files where the size savings are usually invisible.
