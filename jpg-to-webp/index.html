Convert JPG to WebP Online for Free

JPG is the most widely used image format on the web, but it is large and inefficient. WebP — Google's modern image format — compresses JPG files 25–35% smaller at identical visual quality, which means faster pages, lower bandwidth, and a better score on Google's Core Web Vitals (LCP). This free tool converts as many JPG/JPEG images as you need to WebP, all inside your browser.

Why convert JPG to WEBP?

Smaller files. WebP uses both lossy and lossless compression to beat JPG at any quality level. Faster websites. Smaller images load quicker, improving user experience and SEO. Transparency support. Unlike JPG, WebP supports transparent backgrounds. Universal support. WebP now works in every major browser, from Chrome and Edge to Safari and Firefox.

Is JPG to WebP conversion private?

Yes — 100%. The entire conversion runs locally on your device. Your images are processed with the HTML5 Canvas API and are never uploaded, tracked, or stored on any server. That means you can safely convert even sensitive or personal images.
