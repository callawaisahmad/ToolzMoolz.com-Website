How to Design & Print Your Custom Business Letterhead

Our free online Business Letterhead Generator is a powerful, simple way to create professional branded stationary. With customizable headers, color pickers, logo support, and elegant font options, you can design a beautiful template for invoices, corporate notifications, or employment letters. The tool runs 100% inside your browser, allowing you to preview and print or save your letterhead as a vector-perfect PDF instantly.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Company Profile: Fill in your Company Name, Slogan, and upload your high-resolution PNG logo file.
  2. Contact Details: Add your business address, official phone number, email address, and official website URL.
  3. Design Customization: Select your brand's header color, accent bar color, and body text color using the color swatches. Choose from Inter, Georgia, Times New Roman, or Courier New font styles.
  4. Interactive Review: View your live-updating design instantly in the "Live Preview" panel.
  5. Export Options: Click **Print** to open your browser's print utility, or tap **Save as PDF** to generate a clean digital document.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my business details kept safe and private?

Absolutely. All inputs, text bindings, and logo images are kept strictly local inside your browser using JavaScript variables. No data is ever transmitted to a server or stored in a database, ensuring 100% privacy.

Can I change the fonts or margins of the template?

Yes! You can toggle between modern sans-serif, classic Georgia serif, formal Times New Roman, or retro typewriter monospace fonts. Margins are optimized for standard A4 and US Letter printing sizes.

How do I download the letterhead as a PDF?

Tap "Save as PDF". This opens your system's native Print dialog. In the Destination dropdown, simply select "Save as PDF" (or Microsoft Print to PDF) and click Save to download your high-quality file.
