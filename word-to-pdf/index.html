How to Convert Word to PDF Online Free

Turn any Word (.docx) document into a polished, print-ready PDF without installing Microsoft Office. Drop your files into the converter, press convert, and then choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. Because your document is rendered by your own browser's PDF engine, the text in the finished file is real vector text — selectable, searchable and copyable — with headings, paragraphs, tables, lists and images preserved and proper A4 page breaks. Nothing is uploaded: your file is read and rendered entirely on your own device, which makes this one of the fastest and most private ways to convert Word to PDF online. It is ideal for resumes, invoices, contracts, client proposals and university assignments where a clean, universally readable format matters.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Select File: Click "Upload from your computer." or drag and drop any Word documents. Each file appears with its name, size and a read progress bar.
  2. Convert to PDF: Click Convert to PDF and wait while each document is prepared in your browser.
  3. Save as PDF: Press Save PDF on a document (or Save all to merge several into one file). In the print dialog that opens, pick Save as PDF as the destination, keep the margins on Default, and press Save. To get one automatic download instead of a dialog, choose Save as PDF directly as the printer.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my Word document private when I convert it to PDF?

Yes. The entire conversion happens locally in your browser. Your .docx file is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere — the progress bars simply show your browser reading and laying out the file.

Is the text in the resulting PDF selectable and searchable?

Yes. The PDF is produced by your browser's own print engine, so the words are stored as real text rather than as a flat picture of the page. You can select, copy and search the text, and the file stays small because it is not storing full-page images.

Will my formatting be preserved?

Standard formatting — fonts, headings, paragraphs, lists, tables and images — carries over cleanly, with proper A4 page breaks. Highly complex layouts like tracked changes, embedded macros, exact multi-column layouts or Word-only effects may be simplified.

Why does a print dialog appear instead of downloading straight away?

Because we refuse to send your document to a server, the saving step is performed by your own browser. In that dialog choose Save as PDF as the destination and press Save. Leave margins on Default so the A4 layout is respected.

What file types are supported?

This converter accepts Microsoft Word documents in the modern .docx format. You can add several files at once, save them individually, or use Save all to merge them into a single PDF. Files up to 25MB each are supported, completely free.
