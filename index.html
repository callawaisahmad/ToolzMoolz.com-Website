Fast, Secure & 100% Free Browser-Based Web Utilities

Welcome to ToolzMoolz, your ultimate digital pocketknife for daily productivity. We build vector-perfect, client-side tools designed to make formatting, cropping, converting, and document signing simple and seamless. Unlike other web tools that force you to create accounts or upload private files to server folders, ToolzMoolz operates 100% locally within your web browser. Your files never leave your device.

🔒 Are my files and images secure?

Absolutely. All operations (such as cropping passport photos, converting messy text into Excel sheets, or placing signatures on PDFs) are executed directly in your browser using secure client-side JavaScript. No data, files, or images are ever uploaded to our servers. We store zero personal data.

🆓 Why is ToolzMoolz completely free?

We believe that core productivity utilities should be universally accessible. There are no registration forms, no payment walls, and no usage credits. If you find our browser tools helpful, you can tip us via Ko-fi to help cover domain and hosting costs, but every tool remains free forever.

⚡ Do I need to install any apps or browser extensions?

No installation or downloads are required. ToolzMoolz works directly in any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile (iOS & Android). Just click on a tool and get instant results.

🪪 What standard passport size dimensions do you support?

Our passport photo cropping tool features standard dimensions for over 40 countries, including the UK (35x45mm), USA & OCI (2x2 inches / 51x51mm), Canada (50x70mm), Australia (35x45mm), Germany, India, Pakistan, and more. You can also specify custom dimensions in millimeters.
