Convert WebP to PNG Online for Free

WebP is a modern, highly compressed web format, but many design tools, printers and older applications still can't open it. PNG is the universal lossless standard that works everywhere. This free tool converts as many WebP images to PNG as you need, right in your browser, preserving full quality and any transparency.

Why convert WebP to PNG?

Maximum compatibility. PNG opens in every image editor, email client and system. Lossless quality. PNG keeps pixel-perfect detail for logos, graphics and screenshots. Transparency preserved. Transparent PNGs stay transparent. It's the safe choice whenever a file needs to be shared, edited or printed.

Is WebP to PNG conversion private?

Yes — 100%. Everything runs locally on your device using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images are never uploaded, tracked, or stored on any server.
