How to Convert HEIC to JPG Online Free

Apple devices save photos in high-efficiency HEIC format by default. That keeps quality high with smaller files — but it causes headaches the moment you try to email a photo, upload to a website, or open it on Windows or Android. Our HEIC to JPG converter decodes your HEIC photos right in the browser and re-encodes them as universal JPEGs, keeping visual quality while ensuring they open everywhere. Since all decoding happens locally, your personal photos never leave your device. Convert iPhone photo batches in seconds and download them one by one or as a single ZIP.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Select Photos: Click the upload box or drag your .heic files into the drop zone — multiple files are supported.
  2. Automatic Conversion: Each photo is decoded and re-encoded as a JPG right in your browser.
  3. Preview: All converted images appear as thumbnails with their file sizes.
  4. Download: Save each JPG individually or grab them all in one ZIP.
