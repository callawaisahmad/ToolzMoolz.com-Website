How to Use the Free Online Case Converter

Our free online case converter tool is designed to quickly format messy blocks of text without forcing you to retype them manually. Copying text from PDFs, emails, or other digital files often breaks typography—our utility makes it effortless to sanitize your content in one click. It is also handy for normalising a keyword list exported from the Keyword Spinner before you upload it to a rank tracker or ad platform.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Input: Paste or type your text inside the "Input Text" panel. You will instantly see your character, word, sentence, and line counts updated in real-time.
  2. Select Format: Tap any of our 12 formatting chips at the top of the panel:
    • UPPER / lower: Standard capitalization.
    • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word (perfect for book titles and headings).
    • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of sentences (perfect for editing prose).
    • camelCase / PascalCase: Removes spaces and joins words with capitals (essential for software developers).
    • snake_case / kebab-case: Joins words with underscores or hyphens (essential for SEO-friendly URLs).
  3. Export: Tap "Copy" to send the output straight to your system clipboard, or tap "Use as Input" to apply multiple conversions sequentially.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this tool mobile friendly?

Yes! The Case Converter layout is fully responsive and optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop displays. You can format large lists or single paragraphs on any device.

Can I revert the toggle case back to standard text?

Yes. Simply copy your toggled output, tap "Use as Input", and apply "Sentence case" or "lower case" to restore readability in seconds.

Does the tool track my word count?

No. The stats display (Word count, sentence count, spaces excluded) runs locally via instant client-side regular expressions. No data is stored, tracked, or sent anywhere.
