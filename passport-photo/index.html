How to Use the Free Online Passport Size Photo Maker

Our free online passport size photo crop tool makes it simple to prepare high-resolution, compliant photos for your official documents. Whether you need a 2x2 inch print for a US visa, a 35x45mm crop for a UK passport, or a customized dimension for standard ID cards across the globe, this tool runs entirely inside your browser for total safety and privacy.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Upload: Click "Choose Photo" or drag-and-drop any standard picture (JPG, PNG, or WEBP) under 10MB.
  2. Select Country Preset: Choose your target country from the standard preset dropdown list. We support dimensions for over 40 countries, including the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, India, Australia, Germany, and more.
  3. Reposition & Zoom: Use your mouse or touch controls to drag the photo within the cropping area. Adjust the **Zoom** slider to properly fit the face guidelines.
  4. Set Background Color: Choose the official background color recommended by your local government agency (e.g. pure white, light blue, or light grey swatches).
  5. Export: Tap **Download Photo** to save your single passport photo or **Download Sheet (8×)** to get a print-ready grid sheet to print on standard 4x6 inch paper.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my personal data uploaded to any servers?

Never. All operations, rendering, and background changes run fully client-side inside your browser via the HTML5 canvas API. No images or files are ever sent over the network, providing complete data security.

What paper size should I use to print the 8-photo sheet?

The 8-photo grid sheet is optimized for standard 4x6 inch (10x15 cm) glossy photo paper. Make sure to set your print scale settings to 100% (do not fit-to-page) to maintain exact millimeter dimensions.

What are the standard passport photo sizes?

UK, Australia, India, Germany, and EU countries typically use 35x45 mm. The United States and OCI use 2x2 inches (51x51 mm), and Canada uses 50x70 mm. Custom presets can also be configured using the custom mm dimensions inputs.
