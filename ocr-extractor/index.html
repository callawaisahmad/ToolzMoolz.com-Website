How to use the OCR Text Extractor

Upload or drag-and-drop any image containing text - a screenshot, document scan, photo of a whiteboard, or anything else. Select the document language and hit Start OCR. The engine runs entirely in your browser, so nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

Is it private and secure?

Yes. The OCR processing happens entirely in your browser using Tesseract.js. No image data is sent to any external server. Your documents stay on your device at all times.
