About ToolzMoolz

ToolzMoolz is a free collection of 30+ browser-based utilities for documents, images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and everyday text tasks. No sign-ups, no uploads, no watermarks, no limits—open a tool, drop your file in, and get the result in seconds.

Why ToolzMoolz Exists

Most online converters ask for an account, queue your file on someone else’s server, and then nag you to upgrade. We built the opposite: every tool runs as JavaScript inside your own browser, which means your files never leave your device and the tools stay instant even on a slow connection.

That design also means there is nothing to break. Because we never see your documents, we cannot lose them, leak them, or lock them behind a paywall. The site works the same in New York, Nairobi, or Lahore, on a ten-year-old laptop or a brand-new phone.

What You Can Do Here

Convert and compress images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, SVG), work with PDFs (merge, split, sign, compress, convert to Word or Excel), turn documents and CSV text into each other, generate QR codes and passport-size photos, trim audio, transcribe voice, clean up AI prompts, and convert text case—plus developer tools like a diff checker, JSON/CSV studio, and MD editor.

How We Make Money

The tools are free and will stay free. The site is funded by a small number of clearly marked advertisements served by Google AdSense and by voluntary tips through Ko-fi. Advertising never blocks a tool, never sits on top of a download button, and never replaces the content you came for. If you prefer, you can refuse advertising cookies with the Cookie Settings link in the footer and keep using everything.

Our Principles

  • Privacy first. Files are processed locally; we never see them. Read the Privacy Policy.
  • No accounts. Nothing to remember, nothing to breach.
  • No dark patterns. Rejecting cookies does not remove features, and there is no fake countdown or hidden trial.
  • Plain-English policies. Short Cookie Policy, readable Terms of Service.

Who Runs It

ToolzMoolz is an independent project run by a small developer team. We answer every reasonable bug report and feature request ourselves. Reach us at dev@toolzmoolz.com, or use the Contact page for the details that help us reply quickly. If you find a tool useful, a coffee through the tip button in the footer keeps the servers—and the developers—going.
