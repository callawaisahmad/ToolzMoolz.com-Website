    Free Keyword Spinner: How to Spin Keywords for SEO

    This free keyword spinner takes one seed keyword and spins it into hundreds of keyword variations, related keywords and long-tail search terms in any language — a keyword generator built for SEO, PPC and content briefs. Everything runs locally in your browser, so your keyword research never leaves your device, and every list exports as TXT or CSV in one click. Searchers do not only type the phrase you sell: they add “near me”, “open now”, “emergency”, “cheap”, “best” and their city name, and each of those phrasings is a separate keyword you can rank for.

    Step-by-Step Instructions:

    1. Enter your keyword: type one seed phrase in any language, for example “Truck Tire Repair London”.
    2. Set language and location: leave language on Auto-detect, or force it manually, and add a city so local modifiers are included.
    3. Choose your modifiers: switch on proximity, 24 hours, business type, mobile, quality and price, and location to steer the tone of the results.
    4. Spin the keywords: press Spin Keywords to build at least 50 main variations plus related service groups, then Shuffle / Regenerate to reshuffle them.
    5. Copy or download: take each list as comma-separated text, one keyword per line, a .txt file or a .csv file.

    Because the output is plain text, it drops straight into the rest of your workflow: clean up inconsistent letter casing with the free Case Converter, load the list into the Text to Excel converter when you want one keyword per row in a spreadsheet, or use the AI Prompt Enhancer & Cleaner to turn a shortlist into a content brief an AI writer can follow.

    Why one seed keyword is never enough for SEO

    A single seed keyword only covers one phrasing of one search. Real queries carry modifiers that change both the competition and the intent behind them, and each combination is its own long-tail keyword. Google’s SEO starter guide puts it simply: build your pages around the words people actually search for. The wider your list of keyword variations, the more of those searches you have a page ready to rank for — spin once, keep the variations you intend to target, and drop the rest.

    Where to use your spun keyword list

    Download the .csv and upload it to Google Keyword Planner to check search volume and bid estimates, or keep the .txt version for a rank tracker, an ad group build sheet or your editorial calendar. Most teams spin the same seed keyword once for SEO and once for PPC, then split the results by modifier group: location and urgency terms usually belong on service pages, while question and long-tail terms are the better fit for blog posts and FAQs.

    Frequently Asked Questions About the Keyword Spinner

    What is a keyword spinner?

    A keyword spinner — sometimes called a keyword generator — takes one seed keyword and writes out the many ways real people search for it: near me, open now, cheap, best, emergency, 24 hours, city names and service synonyms. Paste your seed phrase and this free keyword variation generator returns the whole set at once.

    Is this keyword variation generator really free?

    Yes. It is a free online tool with no sign-up, no credits and no watermark. Every feature of this keyword variation generator — Spin, Shuffle, copy and download — works the moment the page loads.

    Do you upload my keywords anywhere?

    No. The rule-based engine runs entirely inside your browser, so nothing is sent to a server unless you deliberately switch on AI mode. Your keyword research stays private.

    How many keyword variations will I get?

    The engine always aims for at least 50 main variations for a single seed keyword, then adds related service groups on top. You can cap the list at 100, 200, 500 or 1000 keywords, or keep the full output.

    Can I download the results as a file?

    Yes. Every list can be copied as a comma-separated string or one keyword per line, and downloaded as a .txt or .csv file for Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or a spreadsheet.
