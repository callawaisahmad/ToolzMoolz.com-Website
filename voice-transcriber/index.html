🎙️ In-Browser Voice Transcriber

Speak into your microphone or play audio locally to generate live transcripts and subtitles.

Status: Ready for speech

How to use the Voice Transcriber

Click Start Recording and speak into your microphone. Your words appear as text in real-time inside the textarea. Use Copy Transcript to grab the text or Download .TXT File to save it locally.

Is it private and secure?

Yes. The transcription is performed entirely in your browser using the Web Speech Recognition API. No audio is ever uploaded, tracked or stored on any server.
