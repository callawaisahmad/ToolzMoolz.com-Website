How to use the EXIF Privacy Cleaner

Drag and drop or click to upload any photo. The tool instantly inspects your image for hidden EXIF metadata including GPS coordinates, camera model, and other device information. When you're ready, hit Download Cleaned & Private Photo to save a version with all metadata stripped out.

Is it private and secure?

Yes. All processing happens entirely inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas re-encoding. No image data is ever uploaded to any server. Your photos never leave your device.
