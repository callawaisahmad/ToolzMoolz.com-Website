How to Sign PDF Documents Online for Free

Our free online PDF Signature tool is a secure, browser-based solution to quickly sign digital contracts, application forms, and business documents. With zero software downloads or registrations required, you can draw a custom signature, type your name using elegant cursive fonts, or upload a transparent signature image. The signature can be dragged and resized anywhere on your PDF canvas, and the final document is merged and saved locally in seconds.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Upload PDF: Tap **Upload PDF** or drag-and-drop your target file into the viewer panel.
  2. Select Signature Method: Choose one of our three convenient methods:
    • ✏️ Draw: Draw your signature directly on the digital pad using your mouse or touchscreen. Choose your pen size and color.
    • 📷 Upload: Upload an image of your signature (transparent PNG files are highly recommended).
    • 🔤 Type: Enter your name and choose from beautiful cursive fonts (Georgia, Brush Script, Dancing Script, Pacifico).
  3. Place on PDF: Tap **Place on PDF** to inject the signature. Drag it to your signature line, and scale the width using our sizing slider or handles.
  4. Export: Verify your placement across pages and click **Download Signed PDF** to save your document.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my private PDF documents secure on ToolzMoolz?

Yes! We use PDF.js and jsPDF libraries strictly client-side. The file is never sent to our servers or processed over the network. Your sensitive business agreements, legal contracts, and personal info remain 100% private and protected.

Can I sign multi-page PDF documents?

Yes! The page navigation bar allows you to step through all pages of your loaded document, place signatures on any page, and compile the entire document into a single signed PDF file.

Is my signature legally binding?

Yes, in most jurisdictions, electronic signatures are legally binding for general agreements, employment contracts, and NDA forms under the ESIGN Act (US) and eIDAS regulations (EU).
